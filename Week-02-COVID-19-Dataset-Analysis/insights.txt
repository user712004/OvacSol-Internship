COVID-19 Dataset Analysis – Insights

1. The top 10 countries recorded significantly higher COVID-19 cases compared to other countries, indicating that the pandemic affected some regions much more severely.

2. The line chart for Pakistan shows how COVID-19 cases increased over time, with noticeable waves during different periods of the pandemic.

3. There is a positive relationship between total cases and total deaths. Countries with higher case counts generally also reported higher death counts.

4. Cleaning the dataset by handling missing values, removing duplicates, and converting the date column ensured more accurate analysis and visualizations.

5. Data visualization made it easier to identify trends, compare countries, and understand the overall impact of COVID-19 across different regions.
